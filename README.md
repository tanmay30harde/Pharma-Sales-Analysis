📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing pharmaceutical sales data using Python, Pandas, NumPy and Matplotlib. The objective is to identify sales patterns across different Anatomical Therapeutic Chemical (ATC) drug categories, evaluate category-wise performance, identify high-performing categories and investigate seasonal trends in respiratory drug sales.

The analysis uses historical monthly pharmaceutical sales data from January 2014 to October 2019.

🎯 Business Objectives

The project addresses the following business questions:

What are the total sales quantities for each drug category (ATC code)?

Which individual drug brands have the highest total sales?

Which three drug categories have the highest sales in January 2015, July 2016 and September 2017?

Which drug category recorded the highest total sales quantity in 2017?

Which drug category has the highest average daily sales?

Are respiratory drugs (R03) sold more during specific months?
